About Bike Trials

Bike Trials is a precision-based motorcycle game that tests your balance and control skills. Navigate through challenging obstacle courses, maintain perfect balance, and reach the finish line without crashing!

Game Features

• Challenging obstacle courses
• Realistic physics and balance mechanics
• Multiple levels with increasing difficulty
• Smooth and responsive controls
• Test your precision and timing
• Free to play in your browser

How to Play

Master the simple yet challenging controls:

  • ← Left Arrow Brake
  • → Right Arrow Throttle

Tips for Success

• Balance is key - use gentle throttle and brake inputs
• Take your time on difficult obstacles
• Learn from each attempt to improve
• Practice makes perfect!
