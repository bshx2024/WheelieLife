About Moto X3M

Moto X3M is an adrenaline-pumping motorcycle racing game that challenges you to navigate through obstacle-filled tracks. Race against time, perform incredible stunts, and try to beat your high score!

Game Features

• Challenging obstacle courses
• Realistic physics engine
• Multiple levels to conquer
• Score multiplier system
• Smooth controls and gameplay
• Free to play in your browser

How to Play

Master the controls to become a Moto X3M champion:

  • ← Left Arrow Brake
  • → Right Arrow Throttle
  • ↓ Down Arrow Perform trick (when available)
  • ↑ Up Arrow Slower rotation change
  • M Enter menu
