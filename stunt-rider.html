1. Sky-High Launch Ramps and Apex Speed

The secret to clearing enormous canyon and skyscraper gaps is hitting the ramp apex at maximum terminal velocity. Avoid making last-second steering adjustments on the approach ramp face; turning while climbing an incline induces dangerous sideways roll that will send your bike spiraling into open air. 2. Giant Corkscrew Loops and Centrifugal Force

Giant 3D corkscrew loops twist your motorcycle horizontally while climbing vertically. Maintaining strong forward momentum is essential: if your speed drops below 60 MPH inside a loop, gravitational pull will overcome tire friction, causing your bike to tumble off the suspended track. Commit full throttle until you exit the curve.

3. Skyscraper Rooftop Gap Jumping

Jumping between skyscraper rooftops requires careful braking management upon touch-down. Many rooftops feature short run-out areas bordered by sheer drops. The moment your wheels touch the rooftop asphalt, slam on both front and rear brakes while initiating a controlled slide to prevent overshooting the edge.

4. Precision Landing Pads and Shock Absorber Physics

Landing from extreme altitudes exerts tremendous pressure on your motorcycle's virtual suspension. If you land nose-first or tail-first, the frame will collapse and eject your stunt rider. In mid-air, use gentle pitch adjustments to level your bike chassis so both front and rear tires absorb the impact simultaneously.
