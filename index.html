How to Play

Precision is everything. Use W / Up Arrow for gas and S / Down Arrow / Space to tap the brakes. Keep that wheel high!

Wheelie Life FAQ - Frequently Asked Questions

What is Wheelie Life?

Wheelie Life (Soflo Wheelie Life) is a free online motorcycle wheelie simulator game. It's the ultimate stunt game focused on performing epic wheelie tricks, mastering motorcycle balance physics, and competing on global leaderboards. Experience realistic bike physics in a vibrant urban setting.

How do I play Wheelie Life?

Playing Wheelie Life is simple: Use W or Up Arrow to accelerate your motorcycle, and S, Down Arrow, or Space to brake and balance. The key is mastering the perfect wheelie balance to build combos and achieve high scores in this addictive wheelie game.

Is Wheelie Life free to play?

Yes! Wheelie Life is 100% free to play. This motorcycle wheelie simulator game runs completely in your web browser with no downloads, no installation, and no hidden costs. Just open and start performing wheelie stunts instantly!

Can I play Wheelie Life on mobile?

Absolutely! Wheelie Life is fully responsive and optimized for both desktop and mobile browsers. Whether you're on a phone, tablet, or computer, you can enjoy smooth motorcycle wheelie action on any device.
